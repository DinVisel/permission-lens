# PL-7702-013 — Relayed authorization

**Severity:** info
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The authorization was found inside a type-0x04 transaction sent by an
account other than the one that signed the authorization tuple.

## Why it matters

This is normal for gas-sponsored account upgrades (a relayer pays gas for the
user's authorization). It is also how a phished or stolen authorization
signature gets used — the attacker doesn't need the victim's key to submit
the transaction, only the signature.

## What to do

If you didn't expect a relayer to submit this on your behalf, treat it as
suspicious and check who the sender is.
