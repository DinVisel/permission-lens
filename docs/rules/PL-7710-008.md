# PL-7710-008 — Unrecognized DelegationManager

**Severity:** high
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The EIP-712 domain's `verifyingContract` isn't a registry-known
`DelegationManager` deployment.

## Why it matters

Every caveat enforcer address is only meaningful *relative to a specific
DelegationManager* — the manager is what actually calls each enforcer
during redemption. An unrecognized manager means there's no basis for
trusting that enforcer addresses will be interpreted the way this decoder
assumes, or that redemption works the way ERC-7710 describes at all.

## What to do

Confirm the `verifyingContract` really is the DelegationManager you expect
before signing. An unfamiliar manager address is reason enough to stop and
verify out of band.
