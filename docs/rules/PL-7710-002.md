# PL-7710-002 — Bearer delegation

**Severity:** high
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The delegation's `delegate` field is the special `ANY_DELEGATE` address
(`0x000…0a11`).

## Why it matters

A normal delegation names one recipient. `ANY_DELEGATE` means anyone who
gets hold of the signed delegation — not just the intended recipient — can
redeem it. This is legitimate for some "open" use cases (a bounty, a public
link), but it means possession of the signature is equivalent to
possession of the authority it grants.

## What to do

Treat the signed delegation itself as sensitive as the authority it grants
— anyone it reaches (intentionally or by leak) can use it.
