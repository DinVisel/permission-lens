# PL-7710-006 — Redelegation

**Severity:** info (medium if the parent delegation wasn't provided)
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The delegation's `authority` field isn't `ROOT_AUTHORITY` — it's the hash
of another delegation, meaning this one re-delegates (a subset of) that
parent's authority (LEARNING.md §7.4).

## Why it matters

Caveats accumulate down a redelegation chain — the true scope of this
delegation is the *combination* of every caveat in the whole chain, not
just the ones on this one. If the parent delegation wasn't decoded
alongside this one, that accumulated scope can't be shown, which is worse
than merely "there's a chain" — it's "there's a chain we can't see."

## What to do

Whenever possible, decode the entire chain together (e.g. a 7715 response's
`context` typically includes it). If only one link is available, treat the
unknown parent scope as an open question, not as "no further restriction."
