# PL-7710-005 — No value limit or target restriction

**Severity:** high
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The delegation has caveats, but none of the recognized ones limit *which*
contracts can be called (a `targets` restriction) or *how much* can move
(a `value-per-call`, `native-total`, `erc20-total`, or `calls` restriction).

## Why it matters

Caveats exist, but they don't bound the two things that matter most: scope
(where) and size (how much). A time bound or a redeemer restriction alone
still leaves the delegate free to call any contract with any amount, for
as long as the delegation is valid.

## What to do

Look for what the existing caveats *do* restrict, and judge whether that's
enough on its own. If not, ask for a delegation that also limits targets or
amounts.
