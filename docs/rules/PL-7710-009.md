# PL-7710-009 — Already expired / expires far out

**Severity:** info (already expired) / medium (expires more than a year from now)
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The delegation has a decoded expiry (`validity.notAfter`, from a
`TimestampEnforcer` caveat's `before` bound) that has already passed, or is
more than one year away.

## Why it matters

An already-expired delegation is dead — informational only. A delegation
that doesn't expire for over a year is the opposite problem from
[PL-7710-003](PL-7710-003.md) (no expiry at all): it has one, but it's far
enough out that it's effectively a long-lived standing authorization.

## What to do

For an already-expired delegation, nothing — it can't be redeemed. For a
far-out expiry, consider whether a shorter window would serve the same
purpose with less exposure.
