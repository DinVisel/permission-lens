# PL-7715-005 — Unrecognized DelegationManager

**Severity:** high
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The response's `delegationManager` field isn't a registry-known
`delegation-manager` deployment.

## Why it matters

Same reasoning as [PL-7710-008](PL-7710-008.md): every caveat enforcer
address in the decoded delegation chain is only meaningful relative to a
specific DelegationManager. An unrecognized one means the whole granted
permission's behavior is unverified, regardless of how clean the request
and decoded caveats otherwise look.

## What to do

Confirm the `delegationManager` really is the contract you expect before
trusting the granted permission.
