# PL-7702-005 — Revokes delegation

**Severity:** info
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The delegate address is `0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000`.

## Why it matters

Per EIP-7702, an authorization to the zero address clears any existing
delegation on the signing account — this is "revoke," not "grant."

## What to do

Nothing — this is the expected way to undo a 7702 delegation. Confirm the
account being revoked is the one you intend.
