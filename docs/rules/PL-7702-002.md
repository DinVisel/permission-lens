# PL-7702-002 — Known-malicious code

**Severity:** critical
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no (address match) / yes (codehash match)

## Condition

The delegate matches a registry entry with `status: malicious` — either by
`(chainId, address)` deployment, or by `codehash`/`normalizedCodehash` once
`@permissionlens/onchain`'s `enrich()` has fetched the delegate's bytecode.

## Why it matters

This is the strongest signal the registry can give: on-chain evidence
(transaction hashes showing theft or sweeping) plus at least one maintainer
approval — see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md). A `normalizedCodehash`
match specifically catches a sweeper redeployed with a different collector
address (a clone of known-malicious code).

## What to do

Do not sign this authorization. If you already did, revoke it — see the
grant's `revocation` field for how.
