# PL-7710-007 — Complex conditions

**Severity:** medium
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

One of the delegation's caveats is recognized as a composite/logic-wrapping
enforcer (e.g. MetaMask's `LogicalOrWrapperEnforcer`) rather than a direct
restriction.

## Why it matters

Most caveats apply unconditionally — every one of them must pass. A
composite enforcer changes that: it can make a restriction apply only
under *some* of several alternative conditions (an OR), which means the
effective restriction can be looser than reading each wrapped caveat in
isolation would suggest.

## What to do

Don't reason about a wrapped caveat's restrictions the same way as a plain
one. The actual bound is "the weakest branch the logic allows," not the
strictest-looking one.
