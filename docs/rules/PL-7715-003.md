# PL-7715-003 — Granted delegation is wider than what was requested

**Severity:** critical
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The response's `context` decodes into a delegation chain (via
`decodeDelegationContext`) that doesn't enforce something the request
asked for — an amount/rate limit for an allowance/stream/periodic
permission type, the requested expiry, or the requested redeemer
restriction. Also fires if `context` can't be decoded at all.

## Why it matters (LEARNING.md §8.4 — the central cross-check)

The request states what a dapp *asked for*. The response's `context` is
what actually gets enforced on-chain when the delegation is redeemed.
These two can disagree — accidentally (a wallet bug) or adversarially (a
compromised or malicious wallet backend). The concrete example from the
background reading: *"the request says '10 USDC/day' but the delegation
has no period enforcer."* Only the delegation matters once it's signed —
trust it, not the request that preceded it.

## What to do

Treat a mismatch as a sign the granted permission is broader than what was
asked for or approved. Don't rely on the request's stated intent once a
mismatch is found — re-derive the actual scope from the decoded
delegation chain (`grant.children`) directly.
