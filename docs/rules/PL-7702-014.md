# PL-7702-014 — Malformed signature

**Severity:** medium
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

Any of:
- `s` is not in the lower half of the secp256k1 curve order (high-s).
- `yParity` is not `0` or `1`.
- The recovered signer does not match an independently declared authority
  (e.g. the `from` field of an `eth_sendTransaction` request).

## Why it matters

A node will reject a non-canonical (`high-s`) signature or one with an
invalid `yParity`, so this authorization cannot be used as-is. A signer
mismatch usually indicates a bug in the wallet or dapp constructing the
request, or an attempt to mislead about who is authorizing what.

## What to do

Treat this as a parsing/display bug to fix upstream, not as something to
sign — a malformed signature will fail on submission anyway.
