# PL-7702-001 — Valid on every chain

**Severity:** high (critical if the delegate is unrecognized)
**Confidence:** certain
**Needs chain data:** no

## Condition

The authorization's `chain_id` is `0`. Per EIP-7702, `chain_id = 0` means the
signature is valid on every chain where the signing account has a nonce that
matches, not just one.

## Why it matters

A single signature can hand control of the account to the delegate on every
EVM chain the wallet is ever used on — including chains the user has never
interacted with, where they may not be watching for it.

## What to do

Prefer authorizations scoped to a specific `chainId`. If `chain_id = 0` is
intentional (e.g. a multi-chain smart account upgrade), confirm the delegate
is recognized (see [PL-7702-004](PL-7702-004.md)) before signing.
